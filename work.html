01Software & product

Ascenda.

An AI-powered daily routine and accountability app for university students. You set your goals, Ascenda builds a realistic routine around them with Claude, and friends on campus help you keep to it.

It started with a personal list — push-ups, reading, one LeetCode problem, an hour alone with my thoughts — and a frustration: I could write the list, but not make it stick. Ascenda is my attempt to build the tool I wanted.

The web version is live, and I’m launching it on the AUCA campus first.

02Startup work

Software for geotechnical workflows.

I’ve been collaborating with an early-stage team on software for geotechnical engineering — the discipline that studies soil and rock before anything is built on top of them. The details are confidential, so this is about the work rather than the product.

It’s teaching me that domain software starts with the workflow, not the code: learning how engineers actually work, where the friction is, and which parts genuinely need software.

It’s also my closest look yet at how an early product takes shape — a small team, unclear edges, and decisions made with incomplete information.

03Education

Teaching English, mentoring students.

I’ve taught English and mentored students. Teaching changed how I communicate: it pushes you to find the simplest true explanation, and to notice quickly when someone is lost.

Mentoring taught me to listen before advising. Often what helps most isn’t more information but a clear next step.

It’s also why education keeps showing up in what I want to build. I’ve seen, from both sides of the desk, how much it changes what’s possible for a person.

04Markets

Nasdaq, gold, and decision-making.

I’ve actively traded financial markets, mainly the Nasdaq and gold, including with proprietary trading firms.

I treat trading as practice in making decisions under uncertainty: define the risk first, accept losses, keep emotion out.

It connects back to engineering. The XAUUSD system above is my way of making my own rules explicit and testable.
