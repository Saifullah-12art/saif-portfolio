About

I grew up believing that opportunity is something you sometimes have to build yourself.

I’m Saifullah Nazari. I’m from Afghanistan, and I now live in Bishkek, where I study Software Engineering at the American University of Central Asia.

That’s part of my story, not all of it. Mostly, I want to understand how things work and use that to build something useful.

Education opened the first doors. Teaching English showed me that explaining something clearly is the fastest way to find out whether you really understand it. Software gave me a way to turn understanding into things other people can use. AI changed my sense of how much one person can attempt. Markets taught me to make decisions without knowing the outcome.

To me these aren’t separate interests. They’re one question: how do I become more capable, and what do I do with it?
