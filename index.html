Building & Growth

How I try to work.

Principles I’m practising, not rules I’ve mastered. Each one comes from something I’ve built, taught, traded, or learned the hard way.

01Problems

Start with real problems.

I’m more interested in real problems than ideas.

I try to understand what is actually difficult, inefficient, expensive, or frustrating for people — whether in engineering, education, finance, or daily life.

The better I understand the problem, the simpler and more powerful the solution usually becomes.

  • Curiosity
  • Observation
  • Research
  • First principles
  • Real problems
02Building

Turn ideas into products.

I believe in building before feeling ready.

A simple prototype teaches more than months of planning. I enjoy turning ideas into working products — from web and mobile experiences to AI-powered tools — and improving them through iteration.

Build. Test. Learn. Rebuild.

  • Prototype
  • Build
  • Test
  • Learn
  • Iterate
  • Ship
03Technology

Use AI as leverage.

I’m fascinated by how much one person can accomplish with the right tools.

I use AI to research, code, design, analyze, and automate work. But what interests me most is not simply doing existing work faster.

I’m interested in exploring what becomes possible when traditional limitations of time, knowledge, and resources begin to disappear.

  • AI
  • Automation
  • Software
  • Leverage
  • Creative work
  • New possibilities
04Learning

Learn by doing — and teaching.

I learn best when I apply what I learn.

Studying software engineering, teaching, building projects, and working with people from different backgrounds have taught me how much deeper understanding becomes through practice.

Teaching has shaped the way I communicate. Building has shaped the way I solve problems.

I try to remain a learner, ask better questions, and share what I learn with others.

  • Learn
  • Apply
  • Teach
  • Share
  • Question
  • Grow together
05Markets

Trade, invest, and think critically.

Markets have taught me lessons that extend far beyond charts.

Trading has forced me to think about probability, uncertainty, risk, patience, discipline, and emotional control.

I’m interested in global markets — particularly technology-driven markets — not simply for short-term returns, but because they provide a constantly evolving laboratory for decision-making under uncertainty.

  • Discipline
  • Risk
  • Probability
  • Patience
  • Decision-making
  • Long-term thinking
06Systems

Build systems that scale.

As ideas and projects grow, systems matter.

I think about automation, documentation, repeatable processes, and clear ownership — whether in software, content, finance, education, or collaborative work.

Good systems reduce unnecessary complexity and create more room for judgment, creativity, and meaningful work.

  • Automation
  • Documentation
  • Process
  • Tools
  • Clarity
  • Scalability
07People

Build with and for people.

My perspective has been shaped by different communities, cultures, classrooms, teams, and international experiences.

From Afghanistan to Kyrgyzstan and through international programs, I’ve learned to value meaningful conversations, cultural exchange, mentorship, and collaboration.

Technology matters most when it ultimately improves something for people.

  • Community
  • Collaboration
  • Mentorship
  • Cultural exchange
  • Leadership
  • Impact
08Long term

Think in decades.

I’m interested in building things that compound.

Knowledge compounds.
Skills compound.
Relationships compound.
Reputation compounds.
Capital compounds.

My goal isn’t simply short-term success. I want to become capable of building things that create long-term value — for myself, my family, my community, and eventually the places that shaped me.

  • Compound
  • Perspective
  • Meaningful work
  • Freedom
  • Contribute
  • Long term

The question I keep coming back to:

“What can I build today that makes tomorrow bigger, more meaningful, and more useful for the people around me?”

I’m always interested in meeting people who are building, learning, investing, and working on ambitious things — especially in software, AI, markets, education, and ideas that create real impact.
